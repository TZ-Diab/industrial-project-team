# Country cards — notes by country (to be checked and updated locally)

Each card follows the nine axes of `multi-country.md` and opens with its **technical standards regime** (column of `standards-regimes.md`). Five cards are shipped as examples; a country without a card is handled with the regime map and the template. What depends on the sector (support mechanism, permit categories, product specs) lives in the sector profile, section "Country × sector notes". To add a country: `_template-country.md`.

## 🇫🇷 France

- **Standards regime**: `eu` — Eurocodes with French national annexes, EN / PED / ATEX, IEC-based electrical (NF C 15-100 / 13-100 / 13-200), Seveso III and IED transposed into the ICPE regime.
- **Permitting / environment**: **ICPE** regime for classified installations (Declaration / Registration / Authorisation by category — categories depend on the sector), handled by **DREAL / prefecture**, environmental assessment and public inquiry depending on the regime; planning (building permit, PLU, ZAN net-zero land take) handled by the municipality / DDT; water law; protected species.
- **Connection**: gas **GRDF** (distribution) / **NaTran (ex-GRTgaz), Teréga** (transmission); electricity **Enedis** (distribution) / **RTE** (transmission), **PTF** (technical and financial proposal) then connection agreement, **S3REnR** regional scheme and cost share for renewables; heat: local networks (concession holders).
- **Support scheme**: arena **CRE** (tenders, regulated tariffs), ADEME / Region (grants) — mechanism by sector (profile).
- **Law / contracts**: French civil law, **ten-year liability** (décennale, with dommages-ouvrage insurance — a strong specificity, often taken out by the Owner); CCAG in public procurement, FIDIC or bespoke contracts in private projects; subcontracting regulated (1975 Act).
- **Tax / labour**: VAT with **reverse charge** on construction subcontracting; posting of workers via **SIPSI** + BTP card; **SPS** health-and-safety coordination mandatory.
- **Standards**: Eurocodes French NA, NF C 15-100 / NF C 13-100 / 13-200, ICPE ministerial orders, pressure equipment (DESP / PED).
- **Language**: French (dossiers and procedures).

## 🇧🇪 Belgium

- **Standards regime**: `eu` — Eurocodes with Belgian national annexes, EN / PED / ATEX, AREI / RGIE (electrical), Synergrid network requirements.
- **Caution: everything is regionalised** — Wallonia (FR), Flanders (NL), Brussels (FR/NL). Procedures, authorities, language and support depend on the region.
- **Permitting / environment**: Wallonia — **permis unique / permis d'environnement** (class 1/2) handled at regional / municipal level; Flanders — **omgevingsvergunning**; regional regulators (**CWaPE** WAL, **VREG** FL, **Brugel** BXL), federal **CREG**.
- **Connection**: gas distribution **ORES / RESA / Fluvius / Sibelga**, transmission **Fluxys**, **Synergrid** requirements; electricity distribution ORES / Fluvius / RESA / Sibelga, transmission **Elia**; **flexible-access / flexibele toegang** regime frequent (curtailable capacity — to be priced in lost volumes).
- **Support scheme**: regional mechanisms (historically **green certificates**) — to be checked by region and by sector.
- **Law / contracts**: civil law (close to French, **ten-year liability** exists); regional differences; contract language to be fixed.
- **Tax / labour**: **30bis withholding** (withholding / declaration obligation for social and tax debts of subcontractors in construction works), **VAT co-contractor / medecontractant** (reverse charge by the client), **Limosa** posting declaration, A1, **VCA** often required on site.
- **Standards**: Eurocodes Belgian NA, AREI / RGIE (electrical).
- **⚠ Substance**: a Belgian SPV run from France (place of effective management) can be requalified — see `multi-country.md`.

## 🇵🇱 Poland

- **Standards regime**: `eu` — Eurocodes with Polish national annexes (PN-EN), EN / PED / ATEX, IRiESP / IRiESD network codes.
- **Permitting / environment**: **decyzja środowiskowa** (environmental decision, **RDOŚ**) then **pozwolenie na budowę** (building permit); local / regional authorities (voivodeship, starostwo).
- **Connection**: gas transmission **GAZ-SYSTEM**, distribution **PSG (Polska Spółka Gazownictwa)**; electricity transmission **PSE**, distribution **OSD** (Tauron, Enea, Energa, PGE, Stoen); a network spur to be built is often the long pole; connection conditions (warunki przyłączenia) to be obtained early.
- **Support scheme**: regulator **URE**; sector mechanisms evolving fast (renewable auctions, recent support frameworks for renewable gases) — the axis that moves fastest.
- **Law / contracts**: Polish civil law; **FIDIC** widespread in infrastructure (often amended); check the governing contract language (often Polish).
- **Tax / labour**: Polish VAT (**split payment** / reverse charge depending on sector), posting of workers and A1, BHP (occupational safety).
- **Standards**: Eurocodes Polish NA (snow / wind different from France), IRiESP / IRiESD (network), units and currency **PLN**.
- **Language**: **Polish** (dossiers / procedures).

## 🇪🇸 Spain

- **Standards regime**: `eu` — Eurocodes / CTE, EN / PED / ATEX, REBT (LV) and RAT (HV) electrical regulations.
- **Permitting / environment**: **autorización ambiental** (integrated or simplified depending on size), local licences (licencia de obras / actividad); competence shared between the **State / comunidades autónomas** (varies strongly from one region to another); DIA (declaración de impacto ambiental).
- **Connection**: gas transmission **Enagás**, distribution **Nedgia (Naturgy) / Redexis / Nortegas**; electricity transmission **REE**, distribution **distribuidoras** (i-DE, e-distribución, UFD, Viesgo…); access and connection permits (**permisos de acceso y conexión**) with financial guarantees and administrative milestones under penalty of lapse.
- **Support scheme**: regulator **CNMC**, **PNIEC** targets, auctions (subastas) and garantías de origen depending on the sector.
- **Law / contracts**: Spanish civil law; regional specificities; ten-year liability (**LOE**) for buildings.
- **Tax / labour**: **IVA** (reverse charge / **inversión del sujeto pasivo** on certain works), posting of workers and A1, **REA** (register of construction companies), safety coordination.
- **Standards**: Eurocodes / CTE, REBT (low voltage), RAT (high voltage).
- **Language**: Spanish (+ co-official regional languages depending on the autonomous community).

## 🇩🇪 Germany

- **Standards regime**: `eu` — Eurocodes with German national annexes (DIN EN), EN / PED / ATEX, DIN / VDE, VDE-AR-N grid connection rules, DVGW (gas).
- **Permitting / environment**: authorisation under the **BImSchG** (Bundes-Immissionsschutzgesetz) — procedure with or without public participation depending on the threshold (4. BImSchV); handled by the **Land** authorities; Baugenehmigung (building permit) and municipal Bebauungsplan; UVP (environmental assessment).
- **Connection**: gas **VNB** (distribution) / **FNB** (transmission); electricity **VNB** / **ÜNB** (Amprion, TenneT, 50Hertz, TransnetBW); **VDE-AR-N** rules (grid code), **DVGW** (gas); Netzanschlussbegehren procedure.
- **Support scheme**: **BNetzA**, **EEG** framework (renewable electricity — auctions), other mechanisms by sector (KWKG, H₂…) — to be checked.
- **Law / contracts**: German civil law (**BGB**); **VOB/B** widely used in works; FIDIC less common; Gewährleistung (warranty) by default.
- **Tax / labour**: **USt** (VAT, **Reverse-Charge §13b** on certain construction works), posting of workers, **Mindestlohn / Zoll** declaration, A1, **SiGeKo** (safety coordination).
- **Standards**: Eurocodes German NA, DIN / VDE, TRBS / TRGS, Betriebssicherheitsverordnung.
- **Language**: German (dossiers / procedures).
