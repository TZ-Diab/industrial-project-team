# 🏳️ <Country> — country card (template)

> Copy this block into `country-cards.md` (or into a file `countries/<code>.md` if the card grows long) and add the matching row to the summary table of `multi-country.md`. Fill in the nine axes; "to be checked" is better than an invention. What depends on the sector (support mechanism, permit categories, product specs) goes into the sector profile, section "Country × sector notes".

## 🏳️ <Country>

- **Standards regime**: the column of `standards-regimes.md` that applies (`eu`, `uk`, `us`, `ca`, `au-nz`, `gcc`, `in`, `cn`, `jp`, `br`, `za`, `intl-og`, `no`, `ch`, `tr`, `eg`, `africa-fr`, `africa-en`, `sg`, `my`, `id`, `kr`, `mx`, `cl`) and the local code names that differ from it (structural code and seismic map, pressure equipment, electrical installation, hazardous-area system, third-party inspection, units).
- **Permitting / environment**: competent authority(ies) (national / regional / local), industrial authorisation regime, environmental assessment and public participation, building permit and planning, indicative lead times.
- **Connection**: gas operators (distribution / transmission), electricity (distribution / transmission), heat, H₂, water; procedure and documents (application, offer, agreement), access regime (firm / flexible), guarantees and lapse.
- **Support scheme**: regulator, arena (one-stop shop, tenders, certificates) — the mechanism itself by sector.
- **Law / contracts**: default applicable law, statutory contractor liabilities (ten-year liability or equivalent), local standards (CCAG, VOB/B…), place of FIDIC, governing language.
- **Tax / labour**: VAT (reverse charge?), withholding taxes, posting of workers (declaration, A1), minimum wage, site safety coordination, local authorisations.
- **Standards**: national annex to the Eurocodes, electrical installation rules, network standards, pressure equipment, units.
- **Language & governance**: language of the dossiers, local governance of the SPV, substance risk.
- **Market & supply chain**: unit costs, labour, local contractors, supplier origin standards.
